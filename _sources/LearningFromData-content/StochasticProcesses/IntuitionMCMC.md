# Intuition for MCMC

Intuition for MCMC includes Why MCMC?; pointers to visualizations (and other local visualizations); random walks; basic MH algorithm pseudocode; Poisson distributions via MCMC example; Metropolis-Hasting intuition; posterior shapes in higher dimensions; etc. There will be lead-ins in various places to the greater details in the subsequent chapters.
